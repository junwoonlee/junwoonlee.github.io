  • Research interests : Spatial Intelligence, Neural Representation, Multi-Agent Systems, Field Robotics

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Robotics at the University of Michigan, supervised by Prof. Yulun Tian. I received my Master's degree in Human and Engineered Environmental Studies from the University of Tokyo with top distinction, supervised by Prof. Atsushi Yamashita. My research focused on LiDAR-visual-inertial and thermal-inertial SLAM, in collaboration with Kubota Corporation. Previously, I received my Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Osaka, where I was supervised by Prof. Masamitsu Kurisu. My research focused on a 3D mapping system for mining sites using a LiDAR-camera system, in collaboration with Komatsu Ltd.
